# PRODUCT.md · Trias Oceanic

## What it is
Brochure / vitrine website (no e-commerce) for **Trias Oceanic**: procurement, sourcing and distribution of equipment for the mining and exploration industries in **Papua New Guinea**. English content only.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Mining and exploration operations managers, procurement officers and site supervisors in PNG.
- They evaluate: is this supplier serious, which brands do they carry, how do I get a quote.
- B2B, industrial, low-frills. They read on desktop at the office and on phones in the field.

## Brands distributed
- **Muster Fire**: fire suppression (mobile plant and fixed installations).
- **Son-Mak**: exploration drilling rigs, mud pumps, tooling.
- **JS Explo / Jinshi Drilltech**: geological drilling tools, bits, rods, core barrels.
- **SinoMud**: drilling fluids, bentonite, polymers, additives.

## Tone and copy rules
- Plain, confident, industrial. Short sentences. No marketing fluff, no invented stats.
- **NO em dashes anywhere** (user reads them as an AI tell). Use periods, commas, colons; the middle dot `·` is fine as a separator.
- Unknown facts (founding year, HQ city, contacts, certifications) stay as `[placeholders]`. Never invent. Do not assert a specific city.
- "Oceanic" is only part of the name: **no maritime/sea/wave/globe imagery ever**. Visual metaphors come from drilling, mining, geology only.

## Anti-references
- Generic green B2B template ("AI slop"): rejected by the user even when technically clean.
- Eco/sustainability startup look (warm bone paper, olive/lime, hand-drawn motifs): wrong for a serious industrial supplier.
- Walls of stacked green sections: rejected. Colour rhythm is white → mist → white → ONE green band → footer.

## Strategic principles
- The design is client-approved and shipping: **identity preservation wins** over novelty. Extend the existing system, don't reinvent it.
- Pro benchmark for LEVEL (not codes): `/Users/sohan/Forex Drilling` ("editorial industrial poster").
